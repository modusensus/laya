# laya-nli-conflict-v10-l1 — research archive (round 10, "remove block B" lever), NOT delivered

> ⚠️ **Research archive — NOT a delivered model**, and **this checkpoint is a local reconstruction, not a kernel-direct artifact**: after a Kaggle output-pull failure the weights were recovered via CRC-verified partial zip salvage, metrics were recomputed on local CPU and τ(noul) refit locally (1.0288). Deviation disclosure: [`HANDOFF_NLI_V10.md` §10.0](https://github.com/modusensus/laya/blob/main/kaggle_eval/HANDOFF_NLI_V10.md). The current production head is [`Modusnsus/laya-nli-memory-conflict`](https://huggingface.co/Modusnsus/laya-nli-memory-conflict) (v4).

Round 10 (2026-09-30 → 10-01) ran a preregistered three-run protocol. L1 = v9 corpus with **block B deleted** (single-lever experiment).

## Headline results
- **B2 passed for the first time ever (0.1216)** — but B1 flipped 0.9078 → 0.2648, the swap-B1 variant also flipped, and the old-20 name-contradiction case missed; both deltas far outside the noise band (B2 −77pp, B1 −64pp) ⇒ **block B is the load-bearing wall for "same-subject attribute contradiction ⇒ true" and must not be deleted**
- verdict 10 PASS / 2 FAIL (old-20 19/20; bias diagnosis 11/14); main val 0.900 (recomputed)
